The Platform
Four programs. One platform.
RPM
Remote Physiological Monitoring
Cellular devices in the home. BP, weight, SpO2, glucose transmitted automatically. Between-visit trends ready before every appointment.
CCM
Chronic Care Management
Monthly care coordination for patients with 2+ chronic conditions. Non-device. Twenty minutes or more per month.
RTM
Remote Therapeutic Monitoring
Therapy adherence and symptom tracking. Common in MSK, wound, respiratory, and rehab.
PCM
Principal Care Management
Enhanced coordination for a single chronic condition. Broader than CCM. Often replaces or bundles it.

New Codes · 1.1.2026
Get paid for partial-month compliance.

Previously, no RPM reimbursement under 16 days of recorded data. As of 2026, that changes.

CPT 99445
2 to 15 days is now billable.
Get paid for partial adherence. Time and care are reimbursed even when patients miss check-ins.
CPT 99470
First 10 to 20 minutes of RPM management.
A new lower-tier code capturing shorter interactions below the old 20-minute threshold.
